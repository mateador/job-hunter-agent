An AI workflow built to fail safely

Drafts job applications for about a twentieth of a cent each, and says so when it can't be trusted.

How it works

  1. SearchRetries transient errors; stops cleanly if it can't
  2. Research rule (code, not the model)Look the company up only when the posting is thin
  3. Draft (one model call)Bullets and cover letter from the CV and the posting
  4. Check 1: structureEmpty or broken output is regenerated once, then flagged
  5. Check 2: groundingEvery figure must come from the CV, posting or research
  6. Save stateResume after a crash; one failed job doesn't lose the others

Every step is written to an audit trail. A bad key or exhausted quota stops the run instead of burning calls.
